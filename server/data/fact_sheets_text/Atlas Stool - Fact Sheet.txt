                    Atlas Stool

                    Named after the mythical Titan condemned to hold up the heavens on his
                    shoulders, the solid legs of the Atlas Stool stand strongly as support for
                    the rounded seat. The meeting and contrast between the legs and the
                    seat is central to the design, where each element exaggerates
                    the other giving the stool its distinct presence and brutalist feel. For Atlas,
                    John Astbury has taken inspiration from several influences in architectur-
                    al history. There are references to porticoes and colonnades, but Atlas
                    builds mainly on the archetypal post and-lintel construction. The Atlas
                    Stool is an anywhere, anytime piece, which can be placed wherever you
                    need a smaller surface and double as a side table when needed.

                                           Indoor

                                           The Atlas Stool in wood finishes is made in Latvia, while the lacquered
                                           version is made in China

                                           Preassembled

                                           The Atlas Stool is offered in both wood and lacquered finishes. The
                                           wood version is crafted from solid oak and comes in either a natural
                                           oak or a traditionally fumed oak finish. The lacquered version is made
                                           from MDF and is available in three high-gloss colours: Burgundy,
                                           Crème, and Espresso. Both the MDF and the solid oak are FSC-cer-
                                           tified, FSC-C205234. The lacquered design is offered in two shapes,
                                           Round and Square. Its sturdy construction makes it suitable as a
                                           versatile stool or side table.

                                           Wipe the product with a soft dry or moist cloth. Do not use any abrasive
                                           cleaning tools or strong cleaning agents.
