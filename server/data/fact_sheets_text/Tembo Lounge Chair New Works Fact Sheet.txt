Product Fact Sheet - Furniture - Tembo Lounge Chair

                                                           Tembo Lounge Chair

                                                                           Nørgaard & Kechayas

                                                                                                        Functionally sitting between a lounger and an armchair, Tembo's
                                                                                                        extended arms invite you into its warm embrace. Its unique form allows
                                                                                                        the Tembo Lounge Chair to be social, while at the same time creating a
                                                                                                        space for reflection. The big, soft elephants ear exterior provides a strik-
                                                                                                        ing form, while the choice of colours and textures can create a playful
                                                                                                        addition, or a bold statement piece.
                                                                                                        About the designer Nørgaard & Kechayas
                                                                                                        The design duo Noergaard and Kechayas present honesty within their
                                                                                                        approach to accessible design, with the only obstacle being their
                                                                                                        creativity. With a strong connection to material and craft, combined with
                                                                                                        Noergaard's reflective mind and Kechayas' action charged approach,
                                                                                                        the duo is focused on problem solving solutions with a strong visual
                                                                                                        language.
