                    Brolly Pendant

                    The Brolly lighting series demonstrates the strength of simplistic shapes.
                    Precise and considered, these elegant lights display the kind of thought-
                    ful, almost deceptive, simplicity that makes them timeless particulars
                    in any decor. In this pendant version of the Brolly Lamp, the conical
                    lampshade is magnified into diameters of 70 and 90 centimetres, their
                    sharp volumes enhancing the architectural expression of the design. The
                    concave diffuser spreads a soft and generous light that does not blind
                    you when you are seated beneath the lamp. Let the pendant hover above
                    the dining table or wherever pleasant light and a significant silhouette are
                    sought.

                    Product highlights

                    -                 Part of the Brolly family, offering a portable table lamp and pendants in various sizes.

                    -                 Compliant with CE, FCC, UKCA, RCM and ETL standards, suitable for use in EU, UK, AU

                                      and US markets.

                    -                 Available in two sizes.

                    -                 Ideal for both public and private settings.
