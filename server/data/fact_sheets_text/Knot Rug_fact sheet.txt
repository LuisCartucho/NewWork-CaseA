                    Knot Rug

                    Designed by Lotta Agaton, the Knot rug collection is woven from soft yet
                    sturdy rope and finished with a simple tied knot, which sits casually in the
                    corner of the rug. Thick and chunky in expression, they boast a generous
                    tactility, introducing layer and weight to the décor. The rugs come in three
                    versatile neutrals and two sizes, where the scale of the rope increases
                    proportionally with the size of the rug, growing from 12 mm to 20 mm in
                    thickness.

                    Product highlights

                    - Handcrafted on traditional pit looms for precise weaving.
                    - Availiable in two sizes.
                    - Yarn thickness is tailored to rug size, with 12 mm for the smaller rug and a 20 mm for the

                           larger size.
                    - Offered in a selection of various natural tones.
                    - Made out of 100% recycled PET.

                                           Unroll the rug.

                                           Handwoven in India, the Knot Rug combines traditional craftsmanship
                                           with modern materials. It is made from rPET, a recycled form of polyester
                                           that is carefully processed and transformed into new, strong and durable
                                           fibres. At the same time, it gives valuable resources a second life through
                                           recycling while maintaining its appearance over time. The Knot rug is
                                           crafted from interwoven ropes, creating an organic form with a subtle
                                           sense of movement--soft and flexible rather than stiff or structured.

                                           Vacuum the rug regularly using low to medium suction and avoid
                                           rotating brushes or robot vacuum cleaners, which may damage fibres.
                                           Loose fibres may appear at first; simply trim them carefully with scis-
                                           sors and do not pull them out. In case of spills, act quickly and gently
                                           dab (do not rub) with a clean, uncoloured cloth, working from the outer
                                           edge towards the centre. Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight
                                           to help preserve colour. Professional cleaning is recommended when
                                           needed. Use a rug underlay to prevent slipping and protect delicate
                                           floors. Rotate the rug occasionally to ensure even wear.
