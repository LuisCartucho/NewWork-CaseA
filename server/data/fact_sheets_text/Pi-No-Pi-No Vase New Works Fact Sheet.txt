Product Fact Sheet - Objects - Pi-No-Pi-No Vase

                                                 Pi-No-Pi-No Vase

                                                 Maija Puoskari & Tuukka Tujula

                                                 The Pi-No-Pi-No Vase takes inspiration from the diversity of nature. It
                                                 can grow as the flowers grow, and change like the seasons. A flexibility
                                                 that means it stays in use throughout the year, from the full bloom of
                                                 spring, to the simple textures of winter. Simply take it apart, or stack it
                                                 together.

                                                 About the Designer Maija Puoskari & Tuukka Tujula

                                                 The clean and wild Finnish nature has been the inspirational backdrop
                                                 to many of Maija Puoskari's functional forms. A strong sense of clean
                                                 lines and honest materials characterizes her pieces. Based in Helsinki,
                                                 Maija's collections have been shown in many national and international
                                                 exhibitions and fairs.

                                                 Graphic designer Tuukka Tujula has worked for some of Finland's top
                                                 studio's and agencies for over a decade, currently holding the position
                                                 of art director and partner at Cassius Helsinki. Working alongside his life
                                                 and creative partner Maija Puoskari, Tuukka's multi-disciplinary approach
                                                 to design is punctuated by a simplicity often inspired by the nature that
                                                 surrounds him.

Cleaning & Care Guide

To maintain the item in a good condition, please clean the surface with a dry and soft, or moist cloth. Do not use any abrasive cleaning tools, and never use
strong cleaning agents such as household glass cleaner, white spirit, turpentine.

Safety Precautions

The Item is made of painted glass and natural wood and therefore it is not dishwasher safe. Do not use abrasive agents as it might damage the surface. The
item is not a toy and is unsuitable for children under the age of 10. Colour changes may occur over time due to the character of the material treatment.
