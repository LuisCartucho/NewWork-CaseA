                                                 Kizu Table Lamp Small

                                                 Lars Tornøe

                                                 Composed of two bold sculptural forms, the Kizu Table Lamp displays a weightless balancing act. The illusion of gravity
                                                 creates a tension within the piece, while its curved forms initiate a calming effect. This kissing point of material and function
                                                 creates an object in perfect harmony.
