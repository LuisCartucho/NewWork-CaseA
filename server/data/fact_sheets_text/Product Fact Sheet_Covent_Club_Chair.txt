                                                          Covent Club Chair

                                                          ARDE

                                                          The existing Covent Chair and Covent Lounge Chair fuse their best assets in the Covent Club Chair. This new family member

                                                          upholstered surfaces and empty spaces create a silhouette defined by both softness and precision.
