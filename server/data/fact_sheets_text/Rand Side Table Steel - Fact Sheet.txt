                    Rand Side Table

                    The Rand Side Table transforms simple geometry into a sculptural every-
                    day piece. Two half-circle plates stacked against a flat surface create an
                    elegant interplay of curves and lines, leaving space for storage between
                    them. Three wide, slim legs anchor the design with architectural clarity.
                    The flat side lets Rand nestle against a wall or sofa, or stand freely as a
                    sculptural presence. Open, easy-to-reach storage keeps your essentials
                    close at hand. Whether used as a bedside companion, side table, or part
                    of a layered coffee table arrangement, Rand brings a sense of intention to
                    daily life.
