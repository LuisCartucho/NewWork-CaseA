                                                     Karl-Johan Table Lamp

                                                     Signe Hytte

                                                     The Karl-Johan Table Lamp is inspired by excursions into nature. A search for wild mushrooms. The tones of an autumn
                                                     forest. This soothing lamp combines the soft curves of glass with the stability of Black Marquina marble or smoked oak.
                                                     The result is a poetic piece, a combination of tone and texture, dark and light.
