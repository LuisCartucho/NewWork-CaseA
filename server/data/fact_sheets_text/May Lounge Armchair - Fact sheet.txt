                     May Lounge Armchair

                     The May family evolves into relaxed seating with the addition of the May
                     Lounge Armchair - a reinterpretation of the May Armchair where com-
                     fort and aesthetics merge in a calm, understated expression. Its softly
                     reclined backrest and lowered seat encourage a restful posture that
                     invites you to slow down and savour a quiet moment under the open sky.
                     The robust steel, finished with a matte powder coating, blends naturally
                     into its surroundings throughout the year, while the timeless and simple
                     design invites lasting use.

                     Product highlights

                     - Part of a family ensemble consisting of different Chairs and Tables as well a Bench.
                     - Features an outdoor powder coating.
                     - Suitable for use in both public and private settings.
                     - The May Lounge Armchair is stackable.
                     - Each package includes two May Lounge Armchairs.
                     - Made from steel for a heavy, qualitative feel.

                                            Preassembled

                                            The May Lounge Armchair is made of steel and features a primer
                                            and top coat suitable for outdoor use. Its frame is made from bent
                                            steel tubes that are welded and polished, while the seat and back are
                                            cold-formed.

                                            Clean the product using a soft, dry or slightly damp cloth. If needed,
                                            apply a mild detergent and promptly wipe away the residue with a
                                            clean cloth. Any leftover detergent can damage the powder coat.
                                            Avoid using abrasive cleaning tools or strong cleaning agents as they
                                            may cause damage to the surface of the product. With regular use,
                                            minor rust spots may naturally appear. Scratches or damage to the
                                            powder-coated surface can make the exposed areas more prone to
                                            corrosion. Chipped or peeling paint from impact may further increase
                                            this risk. Furthermore, placing the furniture near the sea can accelerate
                                            the corrosion process.
