                    Nebra Floor Lamp

                    The Nebra Floor Lamp features the series' signature plissé shade, offering
                    a delicate, structured lightness that contrasts effortlessly with its matte
                    steel base. The adjustable shade, operable with a gentle sweeping
                    motion, allows for easy modulation of both the lamp's expression and
                    the direction of light. The Nebra Floor Lamp even has an adjustable pole,
                    which can be set to any height between 148 and 175 cm, enabling you to
                    adjust the light comfortably to its surroundings and user. These
                    considered features elevate Nebra beyond mere illumination, shaping a
                    dynamic interplay of form and function.
