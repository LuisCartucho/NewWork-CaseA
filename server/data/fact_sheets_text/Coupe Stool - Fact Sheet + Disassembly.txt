                         Coupe Stool & Bar Stool

                         The Coupe Stool and Bar Stool began as a naïve sketch: a single
                         vertical line met by three horizontals, inspired by the reduced silhouettes
                         of classic cocktail glasses and champagne coupes. While the forms are
                         unapologetically simple, Coupe maintains a distinct character. Its identity
                         is defined by a strong materiality and clarity of form that gives the design
                         a significant presence in any room. Despite the solid feel and volume, the
                         precise proportions and balanced geometry achieve a lightness of touch,
                         making the overall aesthetic one of a balanced, classic silhouette.

                         Product highlights

                         - A family ensemble of indoor stools and bar stools.
                         - Suitable for both public and private use.
                         - Available in passivated stainless steel and powder-coated finish.
